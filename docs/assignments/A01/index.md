Product Analysis: Binder Clip

Binder clips are small devices used to clamp things together effectively and conveniently, but in most cases they are used to hold papers together. They can come in many sizes in order to be useful in multiple instances. They remove the need to have holes to hold things together, or any sort of adhesive. The specific binder clip I am referring to was patented to Louis E. Baltzley of Washington, D.C. Binder clips have one triangular metal clamp and two wire handles. The triangular metal body has an elastic spring property(Spring force) to it that makes it return to its original orientation when uncompressed, and the handles can be thought of as levers. The more force you apply to the wires the more the clamp opens thus allowing for larger materials to fit. The elastic behavior of a spring validates the model of binder clips. The original shape of the handles are easier to grip and provide the necessary force to open the clamp
